In this list, the National Law (below) means the Education and Care Services National Law (South Australia). National Regulations means the Education and Care Services National Regulations 2011.

1st Impressions Early Learning Centre

Approved provider: 1st Impressions Education Group Pty Ltd

Address: 179-181 Main North Road, Nailsworth SA 5083

Date of action: 12/2/2020

Type of action: Compliance notice pursuant to s. 177 of the National Law

Details of action: The Registrar of the Education Standards Board issued the approved provider with a compliance notice in relation to failing to comply with provisions of the National Law and Regulations. The matters giving rise to the compliance notice being issued included:

  • Protection of children from harm and hazards pursuant to s. 167
  • Required programs pursuant to s. 168
  • Staffing arrangements pursuant to s. 169
  • Unauthorised persons on education and care service premises pursuant to s. 170
  • Requirement to keep enrolment and other documents pursuant to s. 175
  • Quality improvement plans pursuant to r. 55
  • Knowledge of child protection law pursuant to r. 84
  • Emergency and evacuation procedures pursuant to r. 97
  • Premises, furniture and equipment to be safe, clean and in good repair pursuant to r. 103
  • Laundry and hygiene facilities pursuant to r. 106
  • Requirement for early childhood teacher- centre based services fewer than 25 children pursuant to r. 131
  • First aid qualifications pursuant to r. 136.

The approved provider has provided evidence that they have complied with the provisions of the National Law and Regulations in relation to the compliance notice.

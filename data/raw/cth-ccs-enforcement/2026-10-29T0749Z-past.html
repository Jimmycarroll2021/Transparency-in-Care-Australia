Happy Tone Children's Wonderland

Provider:
Happy Tone Education and Service Group Pty Ltd
Location:
Myaree, WA
Service type:
Centre Based Day Care
Type of sanction:
Sanction under section 195H – Cancellation of Provider Approval
Conditions of approval or grounds for sanction:

The provider has not complied with the following conditions for continued approval:

  • Compliance with rules and law – continued satisfaction of eligibility rules (section 195A(1))
    • Child care service cannot be primarily conducted to provide instruction in an activity (section 194D(a)(ii))
    • Centre-based day care service cannot primarily operate as an outside of school hours service (paragraph 45(2)(b) of the Minister's Rules)
    • Quality and safety considerations (section 194D(f)(va))
    • Compliance with a law of the Commonwealth or a state or territory (194D(f)(ii)
  • Compliance with rules and law – compliance with Family Assistance Law (section 195A(2))
    • Requirement to keep records (section 202B)
    • Requirement to report about children for whom care is provided (section 204B)
  • Compliance with rules and law – cooperation with a person exercising powers under section 154 (section 195A(3))
  • Compliance with rules and law – compliance with Commonwealth, state and territory laws (section 195A(4))
    • Compliance with all requirements imposed by a law of the Commonwealth or a law of the state or territory in which the service is situated.

As a result, cancellation of the provider’s approval, so that the provider is no longer approved in respect of one or more of its services, was determined to be the appropriate sanction in respect of the non-compliance.

Date of effect:
17 December 2025

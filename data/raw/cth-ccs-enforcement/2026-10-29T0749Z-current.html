Enforcement action register

We take enforcement action against early childhood education and care providers and services who do not comply with Family Assistance Law. We may publish details of our enforcement action in the public interest.

On this page:

Enforcement actions we may publish can include:

  • suspending or cancelling your CCS approval
  • civil penalty orders
  • conditions imposed for continued approval
  • criminal convictions.

We may publish information about our enforcement action, in accordance with section 199B of A New Tax System (Family Assistance) (Administration) Act 1999.

This register includes enforcement actions in the last 12 months. You can find details of enforcement actions from previous years on the past enforcement action register webpage.

If you have questions about this register, please email ChildCareEnforcementActionRegister@education.gov.au.

Find out more about your obligations as an approved provider and the consequences of not complying on our compliance and enforcement page.

If you become aware of illegal or fraudulent activity in the early childhood education and care sector, you can report it anonymously.

# Rules for anything the public will see

Defamation and child-protection law apply. Get Australian legal advice before launch.

- Publish verified regulatory facts only. Each fact shows its source, its date and a link to the
  primary record.
- No allegations, media claims or inferences about named providers or services.
- Never name individuals.
- Every count shows a denominator and a time window, and a sector comparison where one exists.
- Label action types exactly: breach, confirmed breach, enforcement action, prosecution, conviction.
- State wherever flags appear that absence of a flag is not evidence of safety.
- Services outside the NQF are 'not assessed under the NQF', never 'unregulated' or 'unsafe'.
- Every provider page links to a right-of-reply and corrections process.
- Attribute ACECQA content as required by its terms, and only once written consent is held.
- WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility.
- Run `/source-check` on any page before it is published.
